A guide to asking/Explain the real problem

Start with your goal.
Then your approach.

Asking only about your proposed solution can hide the real problem. This is often called the XY problem.

About a 2-minute read

A conversation that stalls

SSara

How do I get the last three characters of a filename?

A correct answer might still solve the wrong problem.

A question someone can answer

SSara

I need to find a file’s extension. I thought of taking its last three characters, but some files end in .jpeg. What approach would work better?

The real goal is visible, so a better approach can be suggested.

How does the XY problem happen?

You want to do X and assume Y is the way to get there. You get stuck on Y and ask only about Y. Someone helps with that step without knowing what you actually need.

Explain your goal and constraints first. Then describe what you tried and why. If a particular approach is required, explain that constraint too.

It is fine to ask about a specific approach. Include the goal and constraints so people can give advice that fits the actual problem.
