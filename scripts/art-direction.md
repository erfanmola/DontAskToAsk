# Clear invitation

A question becomes an invitation when its shape makes room for an answer. A rounded field with one open corner expresses that opening. The form should read immediately at favicon scale, with meticulously balanced curves and no decorative detail competing with the mark.

Indigo carries the identity against a quiet blue-white ground. Deep navy provides the complementary night surface. Mint belongs to the moment when a message becomes answerable. These colors have distinct jobs; painstaking contrast and spacing make the composition feel considered rather than ornamental.

Typography expresses the same idea at a larger scale. A short question and its direct answer sit in generous space. Manrope and Vazirmatn preserve the characteristic rhythm of each script, with master-level attention to the relationship between their baselines, weight, and surrounding forms.

The cover is a conversation reduced to its useful parts: one interrupted opening and one complete message. The hierarchy is meticulously crafted to remain legible in a small repository preview. The work gains refinement by removing distractions and adjusting proportion, never by adding unnecessary ornament.
